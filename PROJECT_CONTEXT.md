# Project Context – ReplyDeck

## Product name

Working name: **ReplyDeck**

Alternative names:
- ApproveMail
- QueueMail
- ReplyQueue
- InboxDeck
- SwipeReply

Use **ReplyDeck** for now.

## One-line description

ReplyDeck is a phone-native AI approval queue for Outlook replies.

## Caveman explanation

```txt
Outlook gets email.
AI reads email.
AI writes reply.
Phone shows card.
User taps Send.
Email sends.
AI remembers.
Next reply gets better.
```

## What we are building

A mobile app that lets busy professionals clear email from their phone without opening Outlook.

The user sees emails as approval cards.

Each card includes:

- sender
- email summary
- what the sender wants
- AI-generated reply
- confidence score
- risk level
- buttons:
  - Send
  - Edit
  - Reject
  - Regenerate
  - Later

## What we are not building

Do not build:

- a full Gmail clone
- a full Outlook clone
- a desktop email client
- a calendar system
- a CRM
- team inboxes in MVP
- Gmail integration in MVP
- autonomous sending in MVP

## Core insight

The AI reply itself is not the unique part.

The unique part is the **mobile approval queue**.

The user should feel like they are swiping through decisions, not managing an inbox.

## Primary user

Busy professionals who live in email:

- founders
- executives
- consultants
- lawyers
- brokers
- property professionals
- salespeople
- agency owners

## Main promise

```txt
Clear your Outlook from your phone in minutes.
```

## Why people pay

People pay because they want:

- less after-hours email work
- faster replies
- fewer inbox backlogs
- less typing on phone
- less stress
- more personal time

## Subscription idea

Start with:

```txt
Pro plan: $49 AUD/month
```

The MVP should validate whether people will pay for the workflow before building advanced enterprise features.

## Important trust promise

The product must always communicate:

```txt
Nothing sends without your approval.
```

The first version must be human-approved only.
